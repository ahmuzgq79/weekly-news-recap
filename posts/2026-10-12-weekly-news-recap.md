# Weekly News Recap: Oct 5-11, 2026

*Compiled from web sources; some are aggregators and not every item was cross-verified. Not investment advice.*

## U.S.

**Trump rallies for Ken Paxton in Texas (Oct 7)** - Protesters repeatedly interrupted the San Antonio rally for the Republican Senate candidate, and one person was reportedly assaulted by supporters afterward.

**DHS election-integrity push before midterms (reported Oct 8)** - CNN reports DHS has reviewed tens of millions of records and scrutinized voter-registration nonprofits, while ICE leaders ordered arrests raised toward 3,000 per day.

**Fuel and environmental rules (Oct 8)** - The administration temporarily allowed truckers to use dyed off-road diesel as fuel costs hit records, and the EPA said it will further weaken federal methane limits for oil and gas.

**Hurricane Isaias (Oct 8)** - The first Atlantic hurricane of the season strengthened rapidly and was tracking toward eastern Louisiana and the Florida Panhandle, near hundreds of offshore oil and gas platforms.

**Executions (Oct 8)** - Texas carried out the 30th U.S. execution of the year, a day after Tennessee's attempt to execute Christa Pike failed.

## World

**Strait of Hormuz tensions (Oct 7-8)** - A tanker was struck north of Qatar after Iran warned ships against bypassing its blockade, following at least nine vessel attacks the prior week, and oil rose over 2%.

**Russian strike on Pryluky, Ukraine (Oct 8)** - A missile hit a residential building and killed at least 22 people, including five children.

**Spain calls snap election (Oct 8)** - PM Pedro Sanchez set a November 29 vote amid housing-crisis protests sparked by the death of an 87-year-old woman after a forced eviction.

**Russia plague report (Oct 8)** - Russia denies a second pneumonic plague case tied to a Siberian lab; the WHO has asked for information and the U.S. filed a diplomatic complaint.

**Yemen/Saudi Arabia (Oct 8)** - Houthi attacks on two Saudi airports, including Riyadh, killed three and wounded 36, and Yemen's displaced population passed 200,000.

**France student protests (Oct 8)** - Hundreds of thousands of high schoolers protested staffing shortages, and the education minister pledged no further school job cuts.

## Tech & AI

**AI financing strain** - Firmus scrapped a roughly $5 billion IPO, SpaceX is reportedly seeking about $40 billion in debt for Nvidia chips, and Morgan Stanley estimates AI infrastructure may need about $1.5 trillion in external financing by 2028.

**Model and platform releases** - Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5, its third 5.5-family model in about a month, and Meta and Sierra introduced an open Personal Agent Protocol with partners including Shopify, Stripe, and Walmart.

**Policy and public opinion** - An AP-NORC poll found 64% of U.S. adults say AI is developing too quickly, the EU Commission requested compliance information from 30+ AI companies, and China issued guidance warning against speculative AI investment.

**Chips and enforcement** - A contractor pleaded guilty in an alleged $2.5 billion scheme to divert Nvidia-equipped servers to China, and Nvidia pledged $1 billion over five years for U.S. research.

**Safety and accountability** - OpenAI fired three researchers over alleged sensitive-information violations (they say it chills safety concerns; OpenAI denies this), and Axios put OpenAI annualized revenue near $50 billion rather than the $70 billion previously circulated.

## Economy & Markets

**Stocks edged to records** - For the week, the S&P 500 rose about 1.2% to roughly 7,812, the Dow about 0.9%, and the Nasdaq about 0.6%, while the Russell 2000 fell; staples, utilities, and energy led while tech lagged.

**Fed in tightening mode** - Minutes described persistent inflation, with most participants seeing another hike by year-end as likely appropriate after September's increase to 3.75%-4.00%; markets give an October hike under 20% odds.

**Treasury yields very high** - The 10-year hit a 52-week high near 5.32% and ended around 5.24% (2-year about 4.80%, 30-year about 5.60%), driven by heavy issuance and higher oil.

**Inflation pressure and data ahead** - Michigan one-year inflation expectations rose to 4.7%, oil stayed elevated (WTI about $92, Brent about $105), and September CPI is due October 14 (consensus about 3.6% headline, 2.5% core). Jobless claims were low at 197,000.

**Earnings** - Delta posted flat EPS as fuel costs overshot guidance, PepsiCo beat estimates, and TSMC's September revenue rose about 55%; JPMorgan reports October 13.
