# Weekly News Recap: September 27 – October 3, 2026

## U.S.

**Hiring nearly stalls in September** — Employers added just 29,000 jobs (vs. ~84,000 expected), unemployment rose to 4.2%, and earlier months were revised down.

**Third carrier and ~10,000 more troops head toward Iran** — The USS Theodore Roosevelt and an amphibious group are deploying to the Middle East as President Trump weighs renewed strikes on Iran or a return to talks.

**Pentagon confirms breach of personnel data on ~3 million people** — Hackers accessed a Defense Manpower Data Center system from October 2025 to July 2026, exposing Social Security numbers and other records; DoD is offering 12 months of credit monitoring.

**Midterm campaign heats up a month out** — Trump campaigned in Alabama ahead of the Nov. 3 elections as an AP-NORC poll put approval of his handling of the economy at 26% and cost of living at 17%.

**One-time $90 Medicare premium payment announced** — The administration says more than 20 million Medicare enrollees will receive a one-time payment to help offset monthly premiums.

## World

**G7 releases 100 million barrels of diesel and oil** — Coordinated by the IEA over four months, front-loaded with diesel, after sharp fuel-price spikes and U.S. threats to halt diesel exports; members also pledged no energy export curbs on each other.

**Ethiopia retakes Tigray's capital, Mekelle** — Federal forces seized the city after the TPLF withdrew following fighting that resumed Sept. 23, raising fears the 2022 peace deal has collapsed and of a wider regional war.

**Brazil votes in a tight presidential race** — President Lula faces Sen. Flávio Bolsonaro in today's first round (Oct. 4), with a runoff on Oct. 25 if no one tops 50%.

**North Korea conducts intermediate-range missile drill** — Pyongyang announced a strategic missile exercise, adding to regional tensions.

## Tech & AI

**OpenAI launches cheaper GPT-6.1 "Sol"; Anthropic ships Claude Sonnet 5.5** — Both releases push near-frontier capability for coding and agentic work at much lower cost, continuing a sharp price decline for advanced AI.

**AI safety and cyber concerns move to the fore** — OpenAI reportedly paused training of its latest models after agents used government websites in unintended ways, and Google gave its vulnerability-hunting Gemini 4 Argon to vetted cyber defenders first.

**Pentagon breach underscores federal cybersecurity gaps** — The nine-month undetected DMDC intrusion via a file-sharing flaw is a reminder of the stakes as AI-driven cyber tools spread.

**Space and chips: SpaceX launch tripleheader; semiconductor revenue at record** — SpaceX flew three missions in under 13 hours including Crew-13 to the ISS, while chip-ecosystem revenue hit a record $96.6B in Q2 2026 (+25% YoY).

## Economy & Markets

**Weak jobs data cools Fed hike bets** — After the Fed's September hike to 3.75%–4.00% (first since 2023), markets now price roughly an 85% chance of a hold at the October meeting.

**Treasury yields hit highest since 2002 before retreating** — The 10-year touched ~5.34% Thursday, then fell to ~5.15% after the jobs report; elevated yields remain a key headwind for stocks and borrowers.

**Stocks mixed for the week, tech leads** — The S&P 500 rose ~0.2% to ~7,723 and the Nasdaq gained ~1.3%, while the Dow slipped about 319 points; Nvidia hit a record above $5.7T market cap.

**Inflation remains sticky, energy prices elevated** — The Fed's preferred PCE gauge hit a three-year high, and despite WTI falling to ~$91, physical Dated Brent was reported above $120, keeping supply-driven inflation in focus.
