# Weekly News Recap — Aug 19–26, 2026

## U.S.

**Supreme Court allows parts of mail-in voting order** — In a 6-3 unsigned ruling Monday, the Court lifted a lower-court block on key portions of President Trump's executive order restricting mail-in voting ahead of the midterms, though it did not rule on the order's underlying legality and one USPS-related injunction remains in place.

**Beef tariff waiver to fight grocery prices** — Trump announced a 90-day tariff waiver allowing up to 300,000 metric tons of imported ground beef into the U.S. to curb surging grocery costs, drawing pushback from cattle ranchers and rural GOP lawmakers.

**U.S.–Canada trade war deepens** — Canada said it will impose dollar-for-dollar countertariffs on the U.S. as Trump threatened additional tariffs on Canadian goods.

**New immigration measures** — The administration announced plans to revoke visas of asylum seekers and to charge employers over $103,000 to hire H-1B visa workers.

**Fed's Jackson Hole symposium opens** — New Fed Chair Kevin Warsh delivers his first Jackson Hole keynote Friday, Aug 28, with markets watching closely amid 30-year Treasury yields near a 19-year high and an unusually divided FOMC.

**Dolly Parton dies at 80** — The country music icon died Tuesday in Nashville after a brief battle with cancer, prompting tributes from across music and public life.

## World

**Gaza ceasefire under strain** — Israeli strikes escalated across Gaza after U.S. envoy Jared Kushner's talks with Netanyahu ended without resolving disarmament and aid disputes, even as the UN-backed Board of Peace said Hamas and other factions agreed in principle to hand weapons and control to a transitional government.

**Russia–Ukraine war** — Putin signed a decree letting the state seize control of poorly protected critical infrastructure amid sustained Ukrainian drone strikes, while Ukraine's allies met in Kyiv as Zelenskyy sought more air-defense interceptors after weeks of record Russian missile and drone attacks.

**South China Sea buildup** — China completed the first construction phase of a man-made island on Antelope Reef in the disputed Paracel archipelago, escalating regional tensions.

**Haiti mass kidnapping** — Haiti saw one of its largest mass kidnappings in years, with a gang leader threatening to kill hostages if authorities kill gang members.
