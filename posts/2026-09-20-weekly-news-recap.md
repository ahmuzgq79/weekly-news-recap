# Weekly News Recap: September 14–20, 2026

## U.S.

**Fed raises rates for first time since 2023** — The Federal Reserve unanimously lifted its benchmark rate by 25 basis points to 3.75%–4.00% on Wednesday, with 16 of 18 officials projecting at least one more hike this year as energy-driven inflation stays elevated.

**Gas prices climb as Iran war drags on** — Average U.S. gas prices rose about 17 cents in a week and 40 cents over the month, with crude holding near or above $100 a barrel amid renewed attacks on Gulf shipping.

**Trump heads to UN, then hosts Xi** — President Trump addresses the UN General Assembly in New York on Tuesday and then hosts Chinese leader Xi Jinping at the White House on September 24, Xi's first White House visit since 2015, with trade and the Iran war on the agenda.

**White House blocks CNN, MSNBC and Politico** — The administration barred three major news outlets from the White House, escalating its long-running conflict with the press.

**Leon Black held in contempt by House panel** — The House Oversight Committee voted to hold billionaire Leon Black in contempt of Congress for refusing to testify or produce subpoenaed documents.

**25th anniversary of 9/11 marked at Ground Zero** — All four living former presidents gathered in New York on September 11 for the 25th anniversary of the attacks.

## World

**Iran conflict escalates around the Strait of Hormuz** — U.S. forces destroyed an eighth Iranian tanker in ten days while Iran's IRGC said it targeted U.S. Navy vessels and commercial ships; Houthi rebels seized a Yemeni port and an island near the Red Sea entrance, and drones knocked out a Saudi backup pipeline.

**Largest-ever drone attack on Moscow** — Moscow's mayor called a wave of Ukrainian drones the largest attack ever on the capital, damaging an oil refinery and a residential building; Poland pledged tighter border security after Russian strikes landed within miles of its territory.

**BRICS summit urges restraint in Middle East** — Leaders meeting in New Delhi called for "maximum restraint" to avoid a wider regional war.

**Singapore offers $55,000 per child to lift birth rate** — Prime Minister Lawrence Wong announced large direct payments to parents in a bid to reverse the country's falling birth rate.

## Tech & AI

**Anthropic CEO calls for AI slowdown; OpenAI rules out 2026 IPO** — Dario Amodei warned that within 6–12 months AI could coordinate swarms capable of taking over much of the internet and urged the industry to let safety catch up; Sam Altman said OpenAI will not go public in 2026, citing safety concerns.

**China rebukes U.S. chip-control push; CXMT hits DRAM milestone** — Beijing's Foreign Ministry called Amodei's essay favoring continued chip export restrictions "fearmongering," while Chinese memory maker CXMT said its fifth-generation DRAM is in mass production with 50%+ better yields, giving Chinese phone makers an alternative to Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron.

**New model releases: Step 5 Preview and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash** — StepFun launched a 600B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with a 1M-token context window, following DeepSeek's V4.1-Flash release on September 10, as Chinese labs keep pace with U.S. frontier models.

**SpaceX wins $946M NASA contract** — NASA awarded SpaceX three additional astronaut missions worth $946 million, days before the company's much-anticipated public-market milestone on September 21.

**Quantum coherence breakthrough at Harvard** — Researchers extended a diamond-based qubit's coherence time roughly threefold using sound waves, a step toward more reliable quantum hardware.

## Economy & Markets

**Stocks mixed after Fed hike** — For the week, the S&P 500 slipped about 0.1% to 7,650, the Nasdaq rose about 0.4% to 26,523, and the Dow fell about 1.7%; the Dow dropped 600 points on Wednesday before markets partly recovered Thursday and Friday.

**10-year Treasury yield touches 5%** — Long-term yields hit 5% on expectations that oil near $100 will keep inflation high and force further Fed tightening, a headwind for bond prices and equity valuations.

**Energy inflation is the Fed's main worry** — Chair Kevin Warsh said inflation remains too high, and economists flagged both prolonged energy costs and heavy AI investment as potential inflationary forces.

**Dollar strengthens on rate hike** — The first Fed hike in three years lifted the dollar against major currencies as rate differentials widened.

**Oracle and Adobe lead a light earnings week** — Tech names reported while investors favored companies with durable earnings and strong balance sheets; cyclicals and small caps lagged.

## Looking Ahead

UN General Assembly (Tue), Trump–Xi summit (Thu, Sept 24), continued oil and Treasury yield watch.
